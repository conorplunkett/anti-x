# Anti-Timeline Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 2026_

Anti-Timeline does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any user data.

Specifically, the extension does **not** collect:

- Browsing history
- Tweet or post content
- Direct messages
- Usernames or profile data
- Clicks or interaction data
- Time spent on pages
- Analytics or telemetry of any kind

## What the extension stores

Your blocking preferences (e.g., "Block Home timeline: on") are stored locally in
your browser using `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your device and
is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

## Network activity

The extension makes no external network requests. It contains no remote code,
analytics SDKs, or third-party services.

## Permissions

- `storage` — used solely to save your preferences locally.
- Host access to `x.com` and `twitter.com` — required for the content script to
  hide feed and recommendation elements on those pages.
